About ASE

Where curious students become software engineers

ASE stands for Advanced Software Engineering. We are a student club at Nitte Meenakshi Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, for people who would rather build something than just read about it. We run contests, hackathons and drives, and we are always looking for the next batch of curious people.

Why Join

Because the best way to learn software is to ship it

Classrooms teach you how things work. ASE gives you a place to actually make them work, with people who are just as curious as you are.

No experience? No problem. Every branch and every year is welcome. Bring curiosity and we will help with the rest.
  1. 01

    Build real projects

    Work on things that run, from club tools to event platforms, instead of only submitting assignments.

  2. 02

    Compete and organise

    Take part in hackathons and contests like I-Solve, Code Conspiracy and VibeCraft, and help run the next ones.

  3. 03

    Learn from seniors

    Get answers from people who have already been through the same subjects, tools and mistakes.

  4. 04

    Find your domain

    Code, research, design, documentation or social media. Pick what suits you, or try more than one.

  5. 05

    Find your team

    Meet the people you will form hackathon teams with, and the friends you will keep after college.

  6. 06

    Build a portfolio

    Leave with projects, event experience and something real to show on a resume or in an interview.
